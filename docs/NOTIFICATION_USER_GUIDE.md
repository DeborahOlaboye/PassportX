# Notification System User Guide

## Overview
The PassportX notification system keeps you informed about important events related to your achievements, communities, and account activity.

## Notification Types

### Badge Notifications
- **Badge Minted**: You've earned a new badge
- **Badge Revoked**: A badge has been revoked from your passport

### Community Notifications
- **Community Invitation**: You've been invited to join a community
- **Achievement Milestone**: You've reached a significant achievement milestone

### System Notifications
- **System Announcement**: Important updates from the PassportX team
- **Admin Notification**: Account-related notifications from administrators

## Notification Channels

### In-App Notifications
Notifications appear directly in the PassportX interface. Look for the bell icon in the top navigation bar to access your notification center.

### Email Notifications
Receive notifications via email based on your preferences. You can configure which notification types are sent via email.

### Push Notifications
Real-time push notifications for immediate awareness of important events.

## Managing Notifications

### Viewing Notifications
1. Click the bell icon in the top navigation bar
2. View your latest notifications in the dropdown
3. Click on a notification to see more details

### Marking as Read
- Click the checkmark icon on a notification to mark it as read
- Use "Mark All as Read" to clear all unread notifications at once

### Deleting Notifications
- Click the trash icon to delete a notification
- Deleted notifications cannot be recovered

## Notification Preferences

Customize your notification experience by configuring your preferences:

1. Go to Settings > Notifications
2. For each notification type, choose which channels to enable:
   - In-app: Show in the notification center
   - Email: Send via email
   - Push: Receive as push notification
3. Toggle the entire notification type on or off

## Best Practices

- Keep your notification preferences updated to avoid overwhelming notifications
- Regularly check your notification center for important updates
- Enable email notifications for critical events you don't want to miss
- Mark notifications as read to keep your notification center organized

## Troubleshooting

### Not Receiving Notifications
1. Check your notification preferences are enabled
2. Verify your email address is correct
3. Check your spam folder for email notifications
4. Ensure push notifications are enabled in your browser settings

### Notifications Not Disappearing
- Try refreshing the page
- Clear your browser cache
- Contact support if the issue persists

## Privacy

Your notification data is private and only accessible to you. PassportX does not share your notification information with third parties.
